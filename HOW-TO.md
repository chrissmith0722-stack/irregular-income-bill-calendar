# How to use (irregular income)

## Setup (15 minutes)

1. List every recurring bill in `bill-roster.md` (rent, utilities, insurance, subscriptions, minimum debt payments).
2. Use **due day of month** (e.g. rent = 1, phone = 15).
3. Estimate amounts; update when statements change.

## Monthly rhythm

1. On day 1 (or payday), copy a fresh `monthly-bill-calendar.md`.
2. Write each bill on its due date square / row.
3. When a client payment or paycheck hits, note the date and **which bills that deposit covers**.
4. If income is late, flag bills in the next 7 days and decide: delay non-critical, negotiate, or use buffer cash.

## Irregular-income tips

- Sort bills into **must-pay-on-time** vs **flexible** (some subscriptions).
- Keep a small **buffer** labeled in notes — this pack does not calculate tax or savings targets.
- After a fat month, pre-pay or set aside cash for thin months (your call — no tax module here).

## Out of scope (intentionally)

- Tax reserve % dashboards
- Full cashflow / profit allocation OS
- Investment tracking
